## 4. 🛑 Blocker: the importer's write path is impossible

`POST /open-api/v1.0/products` is a **print-on-demand design pipeline**, not product CRUD. It needs
`productTemplateId` + `regions[]` and accepts no `price`, `variants`, `slug`, `stock` or `images`.

**Consequence:** the originals — needing their own price, SKU, stock of 1, self-fulfilment — cannot be
created by the API at all. Manual products are **dashboard-only**.

→ T12. This is *why* the earlier false-success defect was fixed by **deletion** rather than repair.
